What we text

Only transactional messages to HVAC business owners:

  • When they book a product demo or setup call with us: a booking confirmation, a reminder the day before and a reminder about an hour before the call (up to 3 texts per booking).
  • When they try our AI receptionist by phone and book a test appointment with our demo company, Sunshine Comfort Heating and Air: one confirmation of that test appointment.

We do not send marketing or promotional texts from these numbers.

Opting out and help

Replying STOP, UNSUBSCRIBE, CANCEL, END or QUIT stops all texts immediately and sends one confirmation. Replying HELP returns our contact information. Consent is never a condition of purchase.

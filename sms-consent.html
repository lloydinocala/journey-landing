How people agree to texts from Journey-HVAC

Journey-HVAC is a brand of Ventex IQ Corp · PO Box 1954, Ocala, FL 34478 · Texts come from our toll-free number, (844) 625-1351.

What we text

Only transactional messages to HVAC business owners who book a product demo or setup call with us: a booking confirmation, a reminder the day before and a reminder about an hour before the call (up to 3 texts per booking), plus replies when someone texts us first. We do not send marketing or promotional texts from this number.

1. Website booking form (journey-hvac.com/start and /signup)

When booking a call, the visitor sees an unchecked checkbox. Texts are sent only if they tick it. Booking does not require it.

The checkbox reads:

Yes, text me a booking confirmation and reminders (the day before and an hour before) from Journey-HVAC at the phone number above. Up to 3 texts per booking. Msg & data rates may apply. Reply HELP for help, STOP to opt out. Not required to book. Privacy Policy · Terms

Live form: https://journey-hvac.com/start#book

2. By phone

When someone calls Journey-HVAC and books a call with our virtual assistant, the assistant asks whether they would like a text confirmation and reminders. Texts are sent only if the caller says yes. Calls are recorded, and the recording keeps the consent.

3. They text us first

If someone texts (844) 625-1351, we reply to their message. We do not add them to any list.

Opting out and help

Replying STOP, UNSUBSCRIBE, CANCEL, END or QUIT stops all texts immediately and sends one confirmation. Replying HELP returns our contact information. Consent is never a condition of purchase.

Hi Mike, thanks for booking with Journey-HVAC. Lloyd will call you Monday, December 7 at 10:00 AM (Eastern). Need a different time? Call or text (844) 625-1351. Reply STOP to opt out.

What is
"AI Forward" ?

AI is changing every industry, and HVAC is ripe for change. Most contractor software was built a decade ago, and its makers are now trying to bolt AI onto the side. Journey-HVAC was built AI Forward from day one.

That means AI working throughout your business. It drafts marketing for you to approve, helps your techs read what a system is really telling them, and flags the jobs, customers, and dollars that would otherwise slip through the cracks. It works the way a sharp office manager and a seasoned lead tech would, but around the clock.

Honest by design: when Journey’s AI makes a call, it shows you the reading behind it. Nothing gets sent, posted, or sold without a human approving it. The big players have money and headcount. With Journey, you have AI that levels the field.

*Built to give the small shop
the advantages to compete

The big HVAC companies win on infrastructure, not skill. They have dispatchers, marketing departments, call centers, and warehouses. You have a few trucks, good techs, and a reputation you’ve earned one job at a time.

Or maybe you are just starting out and reality just hit you with impending competitive disadvantages.

Journey-HVAC closes that gap. It gives a two-truck shop and a ten-truck shop the same machinery the hundred-truck shops run on, with AI handling the work you’d otherwise need to hire for.

See what that looks like:

A field app your techs will actually use. Job details, pricebooks, readings, and payments in one place on the truck. See the field app →

Dispatch and scheduling that runs like a real office. Even if your “office” is one person. See dispatch →

Marketing that runs itself, with you in charge. AI drafts the posts, you approve them, and Journey publishes. See marketing →

Maintenance agreements that renew instead of quietly lapsing. Recurring revenue the big shops are built on. See maintenance plans →

A customer portal that looks like you’re the big company. Customers can see their history, approve estimates, and pay online. See the customer portal →

The big shops have had these advantages for years. Now you do too, and you keep doing the work the honest way.

Journey HVAC and Home Comfort Connect apps, by Ventex IQ Corp

Contact us: email mail@journey-hvac.com or call (844) 486-1625. Include your name, your company's name, and which app you're using. We reply as soon as we can, usually the same business day.

Journey HVAC (field app for technicians)

  • Signing in or a forgotten password: your company's Journey administrator creates your account and can reset your password from the Team page. Ask them first; it's the fastest way.
  • Location: Journey shares your location with your company while you are signed in, including when the app is closed. Set location to Always (iPhone: Settings → Journey HVAC → Location) or Allow all the time (Android: Settings → Apps → Journey → Permissions → Location). Signing out stops it.
  • Notifications: turn them on so you get job alerts from the office.
  • No signal: open the app once while you have signal and your upcoming jobs are saved to the phone. Anything you change offline is sent when you're back in range.
  • Phone Setup: inside the app, the menu's Phone Setup page walks through every permission Journey uses.

Home Comfort Connect (app for homeowners)

  • Signing in: use the email address your HVAC company has on file. We email you a one-time code; no password needed.
  • Your service records: visits, equipment, estimates and invoices come from your HVAC company. For questions about a visit or a bill, contact your company directly.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 15, 2026

Journey ("Journey-HVAC," "we," "us"), operated by Ventex IQ Corp, is a field-service platform for HVAC contractors. This policy explains how we collect and use information when you contact us, request a demo, sign up, or communicate with us during and after our sales process, and when you use the Journey web and mobile apps.

Information we collect

When you request information, start a sign-up, or communicate with us, we collect your name, business name, phone number, email address, and the details you provide about your business. We use this information to respond to you, set up and support your account, and communicate with you about our products and your service with us.

How we use your information

We use your contact information to answer your questions, schedule and confirm demos and calls, guide account setup and onboarding, send account and support updates, and otherwise communicate with you about the services you requested.

Information collected by the Journey apps

Journey is used by HVAC companies (our subscribers) and their employees. Accounts are created by each company's administrator. When the company's staff use the Journey web or mobile app, we process the following on the company's behalf, only to provide the service:

  • Account information: the user's name, email address, phone number, and role.
  • Customer and job records: names, addresses, phone numbers, and email addresses of the company's customers, plus job details, notes, estimates, invoices, and payment records entered by the company's staff. Card payments are processed by our payment processor; card numbers are never stored by Journey.
  • Location: with the user's permission, the device's location while the user is signed in to the field app, including while the app runs in the background (an ongoing notification shows when location is active). It is used for routing, job arrival, "on my way" updates, mileage, and worker safety. Field users agree to this in the app's use terms before working jobs, and location access can be turned off in the device settings.
  • Photos and files: photos and documents the user chooses to attach to jobs.
  • Messages: text messages and emails the company sends to its customers through Journey, and notes shared among staff.
  • Calls (Android, optional): if the user turns on caller ID and call logging, the app reads the phone's call history to identify callers and log business calls (phone number, time, and duration). Callers saved in the phone's own contacts are treated as personal and are not logged. Call audio is never recorded or accessed. This feature can be turned off at any time.
  • Voice input: when the user speaks to Journey's assistant, the device's built-in speech service converts speech to text; Journey receives only the text.
  • Device information: a push-notification token so the app can send job alerts.

We do not use this information for advertising, we do not sell it, and we do not track users across other apps or websites. All data is encrypted in transit.

Account and data deletion

Users can ask for their account and personal data to be deleted at any time. See how to delete your Journey account for the steps and what is deleted or kept.

Text message (SMS) communications

Journey offers an SMS program so we can communicate with you during and after the sales process. By providing your mobile number and opting in, you agree to receive text messages from Journey (Journey-HVAC), operated by Ventex IQ Corp.

  • Message types: demo and appointment scheduling and reminders, account setup and onboarding, account and support updates, and replies to your inquiries.
  • Message frequency: message frequency varies based on your activity with us.
  • Cost: message and data rates may apply.
  • How you opt in: you opt in by providing your mobile number and agreeing to receive texts — for example, by checking the consent box on our sign-up or contact forms, or by giving your number to our team. Consent to receive text messages is not a condition of purchasing any goods or services.
  • How to opt out: reply STOP at any time to cancel. You will receive a one-time confirmation and no further messages unless you re-subscribe. You may also reply OPTOUT, CANCEL, END, QUIT, UNSUBSCRIBE, or REVOKE.
  • Help: reply HELP, or contact us at support@journey-hvac.com.
  • Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.

Your mobile information is never shared for marketing. No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. We do not sell, rent, or trade your mobile phone number, your SMS opt-in, or your consent to any third parties. Text messaging originator opt-in data and consent will not be shared with any third parties.

How we share information

We do not sell or rent your personal information. We share information only in these limited ways:

  • With service providers who help us operate — for example, our payment processor, our email-delivery provider, and our text-messaging provider — strictly so they can perform those services for us, under confidentiality obligations and not for their own marketing.
  • When required by law, or to protect our rights, property, or safety.

Mobile opt-in data and SMS consent are expressly excluded from all sharing and are used only to deliver the messages you requested.

Data retention

We retain contact and account information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide our services and maintain business records.

Security

We take reasonable measures to protect the information we collect against unauthorized access, loss, or misuse.

Children's privacy

Our services are directed to businesses and adults. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date above reflects the most recent revision.

Delete your Home Comfort Connect account

Home Comfort Connect app, by Ventex IQ Corp · Last updated: November 15, 2026

Home Comfort Connect lets customers of HVAC companies that use Journey see their home's service information. You can delete your app account at any time.

Delete it in the app

  1. Open Home Comfort Connect and sign in.
  2. On the home screen, tap Edit next to your name.
  3. Scroll to the bottom and tap Delete my account, then Yes, delete my account.

Your account is deleted right away and you are signed out.

Or ask us by email

Email mail@journey-hvac.com with the subject "Delete my Home Comfort Connect account," from the email address you sign in with. We will confirm and complete it within 30 days.

What is deleted

  • Your sign-in account for the app, and its link to your service records.

What is kept

  • Your HVAC company's service records (your contact details, equipment, visits, estimates, invoices, and payments) are that company's business records and stay with the company. To have those removed, contact your HVAC company directly.
  • Backups are overwritten within 30 days.

Delete your Journey account

Journey-HVAC app, by Ventex IQ Corp · Last updated: November 15, 2026

You can ask us to delete your Journey (Journey-HVAC) account and the personal data tied to it at any time. Journey accounts are created by the HVAC company you work for, so there are two ways to do it.

How to request deletion

  1. Ask your company's Journey administrator to turn off your access. This stops your sign-in immediately, and they can forward your deletion request to us.
  2. Or email us directly at mail@journey-hvac.com with the subject "Delete my Journey account." Send it from the email address you sign in with, and include your name and your company's name. We will confirm your request and complete it within 30 days.

Company owners: to close your company's entire Journey account and delete all of its data, email mail@journey-hvac.com from the owner's sign-in address.

What is deleted

  • Your sign-in account (email address and password).
  • Your personal profile: name, phone number, and emergency contact.
  • Your device data: push-notification tokens, location history, and call-log entries recorded from your phone.

What is kept, and for how long

  • Company business records you created or worked on (jobs, customer records, estimates, invoices, payments, and job photos) belong to your employer and stay in the company's Journey account. Your name may remain on those records as the person who did the work.
  • Records we must keep by law, such as payment and tax records, are kept for up to 7 years and then deleted.
  • Backups are overwritten within 30 days of deletion.

About Ventex IQ

We started Ventex IQ after years inside the HVAC business, where we saw good contractors held back by expensive, outdated tools and customers who no longer trust the industry's sales tactics.

  • Honesty built in. Our software shows the facts behind every recommendation, so customers can trust what they're told.
  • AI that does the work. Journey takes care of routine tasks like answering calls, drafting estimates, and following up, so people can focus on the job.
  • Built for small and mid-sized shops. Professional tools at a price that lets independent contractors compete with the biggest names.
